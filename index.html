Our story

甲奔 means “let’s eat”

In Taiwanese, jia̍h-pn̄g is what you say when food’s on the table. We opened Yum Yum Bites with one simple goal: bring the real flavours of Taiwan to Sheffield.

We opened our doors in the middle of the pandemic, and our food brought comfort to a lot of people when they needed it. We’re proud of how far we’ve come since then.

Everything is cooked fresh to order, fast and good value, with flavours gently adapted for local tastes. You can get us on delivery, but we always say: come in. Sit under the mural, say hi to the bear, and eat it hot.

  • FastCooked to order, ready in minutes
